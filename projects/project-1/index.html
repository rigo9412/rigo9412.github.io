Contabilizate

Accounting system for RESICO and individual taxpayers in Mexico: downloads invoices and calculates monthly taxes automatically.

  • Next.js
  • Hono
  • Turso
  • Drizzle
Live demo ↗

📋 Features

1. Download Issued and Received Invoices

Access and download all your electronic invoices, both issued and received. Keep a clear and organized record of your transactions directly from the platform.

2. Automatic Monthly Tax Calculation

Our system automatically calculates your monthly taxes based on your income and expenses, ensuring you meet your tax obligations without complications. Forget about manual calculations and ensure accurate tax payments.

3. Optimized for RESICO and Individual Taxpayers

Designed with the needs of RESICO taxpayers and individual taxpayers in mind, simplifying the accounting and tax process. Maximize your time and focus on what truly matters.

Key Features:

  • Next.js: The backbone of the application, ensuring fast rendering, excellent SEO, and efficient API routes for smooth interactions with your data.
  • Hono.js: A lightweight and high-performance framework, enabling a streamlined routing system and quick responses.
  • Turso (SQLite): A powerful and flexible database engine, providing fast access to your financial data while keeping it secure and easy to manage.
  • Drizzle ORM: Simplifying interactions with the database, making data fetching and manipulation easier and more efficient.

With this stack, we deliver a robust, modern, and optimized solution to help you manage your finances and comply with tax obligations effortlessly.
