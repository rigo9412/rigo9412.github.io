Encargo

An open delivery network with zero commissions: post what you need delivered, a nearby courier takes it and you pay them directly.

  • Product
  • Landing
  • Supabase
Live demo ↗

Encargo is a product experiment for local deliveries without platform fees. Customers post an order, a nearby courier picks it up, and payment goes straight to the courier.

The current stage is a landing page that measures interest from couriers and customers, with sign-ups stored in Supabase, to validate demand before building the full app.

Planned features

  • Real-time order tracking.
  • Courier reputation and reviews.
  • Order audit log for both sides.
  • Dashboard for couriers to manage their deliveries.
